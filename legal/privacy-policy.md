
# Molada Pay - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** January 2025

## 1. Introduction

Molada Pay ("we," "us," "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your personal information when you use our digital payment platform.

By using Molada Pay, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information (KYC)
When you register and verify your account, we collect:
- **Full name**
- **Date of birth** (auto-calculated age)
- **Email address**
- **Phone number**
- **Country & currency preference**
- **Government-issued ID** (passport, driver's license, national ID)
- **Proof of address** (utility bill, bank statement)
- **Selfie/photo** (for identity verification)

### 2.2 Financial Information
- **Wallet balance** (fiat and crypto)
- **Bank account details** (for withdrawals)
- **Transaction history** (sends, receives, swaps, bills)
- **Cryptocurrency addresses** (unique per user per chain)
- **Virtual card details** (encrypted)

### 2.3 Device & Security Information
- **Device fingerprint** (browser, OS, screen resolution)
- **IP address** (for geolocation and fraud detection)
- **VPN detection status**
- **Login timestamps & locations**
- **Biometric data** (if enabled: fingerprint/Face ID - stored locally on device)
- **PIN** (hashed, never stored in plain text)

### 2.4 Minor Account Information
For users under 18:
- **Parent/guardian email**
- **Parental consent timestamp**
- **Weekly activity reports** (sent to parent)

### 2.5 Usage Data
- **Pages visited** (dashboard, transfers, crypto, bills)
- **Features used** (QR code scans, money requests)
- **Customer support tickets** (messages, attachments)
- **Notification preferences** (email, push, SMS)

### 2.6 Cookies & Tracking
We use cookies to:
- Keep you logged in
- Remember your language preference
- Analyze platform usage (via analytics tools)
- Prevent fraud and unauthorized access

## 5. Data Security

### 5.1 Encryption
- **In Transit:** All data encrypted with TLS/SSL (HTTPS)
- **At Rest:** Sensitive data (PINs, private keys) encrypted with AES-256
- **Private Keys:** Stored in encrypted format, never exposed

### 5.2 Access Control
- **Role-based access:** Only authorized staff can view user data
- **Admin accounts:** Require 2FA and IP whitelisting
- **Audit logs:** All admin actions logged and monitored

### 5.3 Security Measures
- **PIN hashing:** Uses bcrypt with salt
- **JWT tokens:** Expire after 24 hours
- **Anti-brute force:** Account locked after 5 failed login attempts
- **Device fingerprinting:** Detect unauthorized access from new devices
- **VPN detection:** Block suspicious VPN usage

### 5.4 Data Breach Protocol
In the event of a breach:
1. Affected users notified within 72 hours
2. NITDA (National Information Technology Development Agency) notified
3. Passwords reset for all affected accounts
4. Security audit conducted and vulnerabilities patched

---

## 6. Data Retention

### 6.1 Active Accounts
- **Personal data:** Retained as long as account is active
- **Transaction history:** Retained for 7 years (legal requirement)
- **KYC documents:** Retained for 5 years after account closure

### 6.2 Inactive Accounts
- Accounts inactive for **12+ months:** Warning email sent
- Accounts inactive for **24+ months:** Automatically deleted (after fund withdrawal)

### 6.3 Deleted Accounts
- **User-requested deletion:** 30-day cooling period before permanent deletion
- **Backup retention:** 90 days (for legal/fraud investigations)
- **Blockchain records:** Cannot be deleted (public ledger)

## 7. Your Rights (NDPR Compliance)

Under Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR), you have the right to:

### 7.1 Access
- Request a copy of your personal data
- View your transaction history
- Download account statements (PDF)

### 7.2 Correction
- Update your email, phone, or address
- Correct errors in your profile

### 7.3 Deletion
- Request account deletion (subject to legal retention requirements)
- Delete saved payment methods

### 7.4 Portability
- Export your data in machine-readable format (JSON/CSV)

### 7.5 Objection
- Opt-out of marketing emails (unsubscribe link provided)
- Disable push notifications

**How to Exercise Your Rights:**
- Email: privacy@moladapay.com
- In-App: Settings > Privacy > Data Rights
- Response time: Within 30 days

## 8. Children's Privacy

### 8.1 Age Verification
- We automatically calculate age from date of birth
- Accounts for users under 13 are prohibited
- Minors (13-17) require parental consent

### 8.2 Parental Rights (Minor Accounts)
Parents can:
- Receive weekly activity reports
- Set transaction limits
- Approve large transactions (>₦20,000)
- Request account suspension/deletion

### 8.3 Birthday Updates
- User ages auto-update daily at 2:30 AM (Nigeria time)
- Birthday emails sent automatically (opt-out available)
- Minors transitioning to 18: Account restrictions lifted, parent notified
