Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Molada Pay

Is Molada Pay safe to use? +
Yes. We use bank-level encryption and security protocols to protect your funds and personal information. Your money is stored securely and transactions are monitored 24/7 for fraud.
What are the fees? +
We charge minimal transaction fees with no hidden charges. Local transfers are free, international transfers have competitive rates starting from 1%, and bill payments have no additional fees.
Which countries do you support? +
We currently support Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, and South Africa, with plans to expand to more African countries soon.
How long do transfers take? +
Local transfers are instant (under 3 seconds). International transfers typically complete within 1-3 business days depending on the destination.
Can I use Molada Pay for business? +
Yes! We offer business accounts with additional features like invoicing, payment links, and API integration. Contact us for more details.
